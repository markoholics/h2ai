Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026  ·  Effective date: 17 June 2026

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to H2AI Technologies ("H²AI", "we", "us", or "our"), operating the website humantothepowerofai.com and related subdomains.

Registered address: 530/3, Kopali Road, Zakirnagar, Mango, Jamshedpur, Jharkhand 832110, India.

This policy is published in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 (including the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data only when you voluntarily provide it or when it is generated through your use of this website. Categories include:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full name, work email address, phone number, job title, company or organisation name.
  • Enquiry Data: The nature of your enquiry, service interest, and any message you submit via our contact or enquiry form.
  • Usage Data: Browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent, referring URL, and IP address, collected automatically via analytics tools.
  • Cookie Data: Small text files placed on your device (see Section 8).

We do not collect sensitive personal data (as defined under the DPDP Act and IT Rules 2011) such as financial information, biometric data, health records, or government identification numbers through this website.

3. Purpose and Use of Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Responding to and managing your enquiries and requests for information.
  • Providing AI advisory, education, and labs services where you have engaged us.
  • Sending you service-related communications, updates, and marketing communications (where you have consented).
  • Improving our website, services, and user experience through analytics.
  • Complying with legal obligations under applicable Indian law.
  • Protecting our legitimate business interests, including fraud prevention and security.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, we process your personal data on the following bases:

  • Consent: Where you have given free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent — for example, by submitting an enquiry form or subscribing to communications.
  • Legitimate Use: Where processing is necessary for legitimate purposes that do not override your fundamental rights, such as operating and improving our website and services.
  • Legal Obligation: Where required to comply with applicable laws or legal processes.

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us at the details in Section 11. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share it in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use Supabase (database infrastructure) to store enquiry form submissions securely. Service providers are contractually obligated to process data only as instructed by us.
  • Analytics Providers: Aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage data is shared with analytics platforms (including Meta Pixel) to understand website performance.
  • Legal Requirements: Where required by law, court order, or governmental authority in India.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the successor entity, with notice provided to you.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Specifically:

  • Enquiry and contact data: retained for up to 3 years from the date of last interaction, unless you request earlier deletion.
  • Analytics and usage data: retained for up to 26 months in aggregate form.

Upon expiry of the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the purposes for which it is being processed.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer (see Section 11). If unsatisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer (Section 11). We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use the following types of cookies and tracking technologies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website. These collect anonymised, aggregate data.
  • Marketing Pixels: We use the Meta Pixel (Facebook) to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to show you relevant content on third-party platforms. This involves the transfer of data to Meta Platforms, Inc. (USA). By using our website, you consent to this use.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling non-essential cookies will not affect your ability to use the website.

9. Security

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures as mandated under the IT (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011, including encrypted data transmission (TLS/HTTPS), access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact our Grievance Officer and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the website following any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
